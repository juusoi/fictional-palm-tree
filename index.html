ABOUT

I work as a QA engineer at Nitor, where I spend my days on the unglamorous half of software quality: the flaky test nobody wants to own, the environment that only breaks on Fridays, the release checklist that has quietly gone stale.

My view is that quality is not a gate at the end of a pipeline. It is a property of how a team works, and the tests are just the part you can run.

Outside work: retro computing, and the peculiar aesthetics of 1980s broadcast technology — which is why this site looks like this.
